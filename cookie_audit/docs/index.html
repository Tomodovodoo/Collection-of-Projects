HOW IT WORKS

Real browsers. Separate decisions.
Observable evidence.

01

Fresh session

Load the homepage in an isolated browser context, capture the initial consent interface, and observe selected tracking endpoints and cookie names before any choice.

02

Test rejection

Inspect visible accept and reject controls, test the rejection path, count interactions, compare prominence, and revisit the page after rejecting.

03

Preserve uncertainty

Retain per-device results and local screenshots. CAPTCHA, missing banners, ambiguous trackers, and unverified rejection paths remain inconclusive rather than being guessed.

Methodological limitations. Browser-only audits cannot reliably detect server-side forwarding, first-party proxied analytics, all fingerprinting, or every legal exemption. Automated visual heuristics and endpoint classifications can create false positives and false negatives. Geo-targeting, A/B tests, paywalls, and CMP behavior can affect outcomes. Raw screenshots remain local unless reviewed and published separately. Source and reproducibility ↗
